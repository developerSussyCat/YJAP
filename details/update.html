Changes to YJAP

An update about the name and information in the archive.

YFA

Update details

← Back to the archive

Shortly after publishing the archives, we realized the name YJAP wasn’t quite right. The archive is mainly intended for ninth-grade students, so we decided to make a name change.

The archive is now called YFA, which stands for YGW Freshies Archives. The new name better reflects who the site is for and what it collects.

We also reviewed the other posts and corrected information that wasn’t accurate. We want the archive to be entertaining while making sure the details are as clear and correct as possible.

Thanks to everyone who shared feedback and helped us improve the archive after it was published.

You can return to the archive using the link above. We’ll continue adding new posts and updates as the school year goes on.
